Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026
The short version: LENS collects nothing. Every scan, category and text search happens entirely on your device. No data is ever uploaded, and there is no account.

What LENS accesses

To organize your screenshots, LENS asks for read access to your Photos library. It reads your screenshots only to analyze and categorize them on your device. The extracted text, categories and thumbnails are stored locally in the app and never transmitted anywhere.

What LENS collects

Nothing. LENS has no servers and no backend. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. We cannot see your screenshots, your search queries, or how you use the app. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

Photos & on-device processing

  • Image analysis uses Apple's Vision framework and the Neural Engine, fully offline.
  • Extracted OCR text is stored only in LENS's private, on-device database to power search.
  • Deleting a screenshot in LENS removes it from your Photos library at your request — that action stays on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. LENS never receives your payment details. Purchase validation is handled on-device via Apple's StoreKit.

Children's privacy

LENS does not collect data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
